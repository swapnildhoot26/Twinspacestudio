3 BHK Interior Design in Dahanukar Colony, Kothrud, Pune

Client: Chinmay Patil · 3 BHK

This home was designed around a client who loves art and wanted a space that felt personal rather than simply decorated. Morning light guides the living room, a pooja unit sits close to the entry, and a window-side study nook was built in as a quiet corner of its own. The kitchen is finished in cream and black; the master bedroom uses sage fluted wardrobes as its main textural note. The children's room centers on a daybed wall, and the second bedroom was designed around twin beds.

"As someone who loves art, I wanted a home that felt personal, not just beautiful. Pooja and Dimple understood that instinctively and created a space where every detail feels thoughtfully chosen, warm and truly ours." — Chinmay Patil

Delivered as a full turnkey project — design, execution, and styling — on our standard 10-12 week process.
