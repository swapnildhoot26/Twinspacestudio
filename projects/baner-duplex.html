3 BHK Duplex Interior Design in Baner, Pune

Client: Aniket Marathe · 3 BHK Duplex

A duplex layout designed across two levels, anchored by a marble backdrop in the main bedroom and a television wall in the living room. A swing was built into the living space, and a dedicated study nook and work-desk bedroom support work-from-home life. A pooja room and generous kitchen storage round out the layout, with a second and third bedroom completing the upper level.

Delivered as a full turnkey project — design, execution, and styling — on our standard 10-12 week process.
