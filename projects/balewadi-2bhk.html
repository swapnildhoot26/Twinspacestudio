2 BHK Interior Design in Balewadi, Pune

Client: Ashish Kadhane · 2 BHK

A compact-footprint home designed to open the kitchen directly onto the living room, with a breakfast counter bridging the two. The master bedroom is finished with a gold inlay wall as its focal point. The second bedroom doubles as a study, and the children's room uses a backlit arch as a design centerpiece. Dining sits against a mirrored wall near a dedicated pooja space.

Delivered as a full turnkey project — design, execution, and styling — on our standard 10-12 week process.

“It is a 2 BHK that behaves like a much bigger house.”

Ashish Kadhane · Balewadi
