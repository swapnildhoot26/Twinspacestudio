2 BHK Interior Design in Balewadi, Pune

Client: Ashish Kadhane · 2 BHK

A compact-footprint home designed to open the kitchen directly onto the living room, with a breakfast counter bridging the two. Dining sits against a mirrored wall near a dedicated pooja space.

Delivered as a full turnkey project — design, execution, and styling — on our standard 10-12 week process.
