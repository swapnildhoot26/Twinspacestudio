3 BHK Interior Design in Kharadi, Pune

Client: Akshay & Radhika Soni · 3 BHK

The entry is defined by fluted panelling and an arched mirror and console at the foyer. The kitchen is finished in sage and white. A guest bedroom uses an arched wardrobe as its centerpiece, while the master bedroom is finished in a soft blush wardrobe with a textured headboard wall. A third bedroom uses terracotta sliders, and a pooja niche near the entry is finished with a lotus mural.

Delivered as a full turnkey project — design, execution, and styling — on our standard 10-12 week process.
