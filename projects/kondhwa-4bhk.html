4 BHK Interior Design in Escon, Kondhwa, Pune

Client: Sachin Sharma · 4 BHK

A larger-format home built around entertaining and everyday family life side by side. The entry opens through a foyer console into a living corner finished in gold tones on cream. Dining sits beneath a slatted ceiling design, with a television wall and breakfast counter anchoring the adjoining living space. The kitchen is finished in glossy grey and black, and the children's room was designed around twin beds.

Delivered as a full turnkey project — design, execution, and styling — on our standard 10-12 week process.
