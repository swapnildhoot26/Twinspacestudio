Interior Designers in Kharadi, Pune

Kharadi is one of the areas we work in most, and it has a distinct rhythm. Towers around EON IT Park and the WTC hand over in large phases, so a whole building reaches possession at once and a hundred families start planning interiors in the same month.

Who is buying, and what they ask for

Most Kharadi owners work a few minutes away in tech or multinational roles. They are usually buying their first or second home, they have researched carefully, and the brief arrives with reference images already attached. The questions are practical: what does it cost, how long does it take, and what happens if the schedule slips.

The work itself is almost always a first full fit-out rather than a renovation, which is an advantage — nothing has to be undone. Open living lounges, a kitchen specified properly the first time rather than upgraded in two years, and lighting that can be controlled without thinking about it come up in nearly every brief.

New-possession towers: what to plan around

Phase handovers create their own constraints. When many flats are being fitted out simultaneously, service lifts are booked solid, material movement is restricted to set hours, and the society is stricter than usual about debris and noise because everyone is affected at once.

The practical consequence is that sequencing matters more here than in a quieter building. We book lift slots in advance and schedule deliveries against them, rather than ordering material and hoping it can reach the flat. Getting this wrong is the single most common reason a Kharadi project runs late.

Holding a new flat together

A bare builder flat has no character to work with, which is freeing and also a trap — it is easy to end up with six rooms that share nothing. One idea repeated with variation does more than six separate good ideas.

In Akshay and Radhika Soni's 3 BHK the arch became that idea: an arched mirror in the foyer, fluted panelling at the entry, an arched wardrobe in the guest bedroom, and a pooja niche with a lotus mural. None of it repeats exactly, but the home reads as one piece.

What a Kharadi interior costs

A full 2 BHK interior runs ₹12–16 lakh and a full 3 BHK ₹16–21 lakh, depending on scope, materials and finishes. You get a line-item quote before anything is ordered, so the kitchen is priced against the wardrobes and you can defer a line if you would rather spend it elsewhere.

For a first fit-out we usually advise putting money into kitchen internals and wardrobe hardware ahead of additional panelling. Soft-close mechanisms and pull-outs are what you touch every day; a feature wall is what you notice for a fortnight.
