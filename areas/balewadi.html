Interior Designers in Balewadi, Pune

Balewadi has moved upmarket steadily over the last few years. The gated townships and premium towers around the High Street and the sports complex draw buyers who want Baner's convenience with a little more space for the money, and the result is a steady market for complete turnkey interiors.

What Balewadi homes are like

The housing is mostly newer — well-built 2 and 3 BHK apartments in large societies with proper amenities. Carpet areas are generally generous for the configuration, and the buildings are recent enough that the services work and the layouts are sensible.

That means the design job is rarely about fixing problems. It is about making a good flat specific to the people living in it, which is a more enjoyable brief and usually a more efficient one. Contemporary panelling, concealed doors and hard-wearing kitchen utilities appear in almost every Balewadi conversation.

Opening the kitchen — when it works

The most common Balewadi request is opening the kitchen into the living room. It buys apparent space, it suits a household that entertains, and it keeps the person cooking part of the room rather than shut away from it.

We will talk you out of it if you cook heavily. An open kitchen means the smell and the noise are in your living room permanently, and no chimney fully solves that with Indian cooking. In Ashish Kadhane's 2 BHK opening it was right — the household entertains, and the kitchen now reads as part of the main space rather than a service area behind a door.

Making a 2 BHK work harder

A 2 BHK has no spare room to absorb a mistake, so every decision has to earn its floor area. The kitchen and the wardrobes together usually account for close to half the budget, because they are the most joinery-heavy elements and the ones that decide daily life.

The second bedroom deserves particular thought. In a Balewadi flat it is rarely just a bedroom — over its life it will be a guest room, a study, a nursery, or a parent's room. Designing it for only one of those is the mistake we most often correct. A wall-mounted desk that does not block the bed, and storage that suits an adult or a child, will serve far longer.

Society rules in the townships

Balewadi's larger gated societies run proper processes: defined working hours, service lift booking, a written scope and often a refundable deposit before work starts. This is a good thing — it means your neighbours are held to the same standard — but it has to be planned for rather than discovered in week one.
