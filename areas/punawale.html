Interior Designers in Punawale, Pune

Punawale is one of the fastest-moving pockets on Pune's western edge. New towers hand over continuously, prices remain accessible relative to Baner or Balewadi, and most buyers are taking possession of their first home.

First-time owners, first fit-out

Nearly all our Punawale work starts from a bare builder handover. Nothing has been done, which is the best possible starting point technically, and the client is usually doing this for the first time, which changes how we work.

First-time owners need the process explained, not assumed. What happens in what order, what a line item covers, when they need to make a decision and what happens if they change their mind afterwards. We would rather spend an extra hour on that at the start than field the same question anxiously in week six.

Budget is the brief

Punawale projects almost always have a firm ceiling, and that is a legitimate brief rather than a limitation. What it demands is honesty about trade-offs.

Our advice is consistent: put money into the things you touch daily and defer the things you look at. Kitchen internals, wardrobe hardware and the entry drop matter more to how the home feels in year three than any panelled wall. A line-item quote makes deferring possible — you can see exactly what removing a scope saves before deciding.

Planning the living and dining together

In most Punawale 2 BHKs the living and dining share one volume. The win is making that read as one considered space rather than two leftover ones, and it has to be planned from the start rather than resolved with furniture later.

In Bhavik Shah's 2 BHK we planned the two together from the bare handover and worked a study nook into the layout rather than adding it once the joinery was booked. The client's concern going in was going over budget; the project closed without unpleasant surprises, which matters more on a first home than anywhere else.

New-tower logistics

Because Punawale societies are new and many flats are being fitted out at once, service lifts are in demand and material movement is restricted to set hours. Booking slots ahead and sequencing deliveries against them is what keeps the schedule intact.

What a Punawale interior costs

A full 2 BHK interior runs ₹12–16 lakh and a full 3 BHK ₹16–21 lakh, depending on scope, materials and finishes. The figure quoted on day one is the figure at handover — changes are yours to approve in writing before anyone lifts a tool.
