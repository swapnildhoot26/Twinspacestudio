Interior Designers in Kothrud, Pune

Kothrud is one of Pune's genuinely established residential belts, and that history shapes every project here. Thirty-year-old societies with generous carpet areas sit on the same road as fresh redevelopment towers, so two homes a hundred metres apart can need completely different approaches.

Older societies: what to check first

The older buildings are often better built than their age suggests, with wall thicknesses and ceiling heights newer construction rarely matches. What they bring is uncertainty behind the surface.

Before drawings are finalised we establish where the plumbing actually runs, whether a wall is structural, how the electrical load is distributed, and what the slab will take. Discovering any of that after carpentry is booked turns a small problem into an expensive one. In an older Kothrud flat this survey is not optional — it is the first real step.

Making a fixed layout work harder

Most Kothrud briefs are not about starting from nothing. The layout is fixed, the family has lived with it or seen it, and the job is to make it work properly — more usable storage, a study that survives daily use, and finishes chosen for family life rather than for a showroom floor.

In Chinmay Patil's 3 BHK in Dahanukar Colony the client is an art lover, so the living room was planned around walls considered enough to hold a growing collection. A pooja unit sits by the entry, a study nook was built into a quiet corner by the window, and the children's room centres on a daybed wall rather than a standard bed.

Renovation while you live there

A good share of Kothrud work happens in occupied or partially occupied homes, which changes everything about sequencing. Work has to be zoned so the family keeps a functioning kitchen or bathroom, dust has to be contained rather than tolerated, and the schedule has to account for the fact that someone is sleeping there.

It is slower than an empty flat and worth saying so upfront. Where a family can move out for the duration, the same scope typically finishes faster and costs less in disruption.

Society constraints

Older Kothrud societies vary widely in how they handle renovation. Some are relaxed; others have strict working hours, no-work periods around festivals, and firm rules on debris removal and material movement through a single lift shared with residents. We establish the rules before quoting a schedule, because they directly affect how long the work takes.

What a Kothrud interior costs

A full 2 BHK interior runs ₹12–16 lakh and a full 3 BHK ₹16–21 lakh. Renovation scopes in older flats are quoted per scope, since the amount of undoing varies — old wiring, old plumbing and old joinery all have to come out before the new work goes in. The line-item quote makes that visible rather than buried.
