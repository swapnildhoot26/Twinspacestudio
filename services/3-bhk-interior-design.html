3 BHK Interior Design in Pune

A 3 BHK gives you something a 2 BHK does not: a room you can assign deliberately. That freedom is also where most 3 BHK projects go wrong, because the third bedroom gets furnished as a spare room and then quietly becomes storage.

3 BHK interior design cost in Pune

A full 3 BHK interior runs ₹16–21 lakh, covering design, materials, all site work and styling through to handover. Duplex layouts are quoted on scope, since the staircase and the second level change the joinery count materially.

That is the average cost of interior design in Pune for this size, and the quote is itemised before work begins. In a 3 BHK the extra bedroom adds a wardrobe run and a ceiling, but the kitchen and living areas usually stay comparable to a 2 BHK, which is why the step up in cost is smaller than the step up in area.

Deciding what the third room is for

Answer this before drawing anything. A study that is genuinely used, a guest room that is genuinely occupied, and a room that holds parents for part of the year are three different rooms. In Chinmay Patil's home in Kothrud a study nook was built by the window as a quiet corner of its own, and the children's room was planned around a daybed wall rather than a standard bed.

Where the room has to do two jobs, we plan the joinery for the harder one. A wardrobe sized for a long-term occupant will always serve a guest; the reverse is not true.

Giving each room a character without losing coherence

Three bedrooms is enough for a home to start feeling disjointed. The way through is one material language repeated with variation rather than three separate schemes. In Akshay and Radhika's home in Kharadi, fluted panelling at the entry, an arched wardrobe in the guest bedroom and the arched foyer mirror are recognisably the same idea in three places.

In Aniket Marathe's duplex in Baner the same principle carried across two floors: marble, teak slats and brass repeated floor to floor so the duplex reads as one home rather than two flats stacked.

Pooja room, storage and the practical layer

Most 3 BHK briefs in Pune include a pooja space, and where it sits is worth deciding early rather than fitting it in later. A niche near the entry, a dedicated unit, or a corner of the dining all work; what does not work is treating it as an afterthought once the joinery is booked.

Beyond that, the practical layer decides how the home lives: wardrobes to the ceiling in all three bedrooms, a utility unit near the kitchen, and a planned drop at the entry so the foyer stays clear.
