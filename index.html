Most projects run 10–12 weeks from initial concept to final styling.

What does a full home interior cost for a 2 BHK or 3 BHK?

As a guide, a full 2 BHK interior runs ₹12–16 lakh and a full 3 BHK ₹16–21 lakh. Where a project lands within that range depends on scope, materials and finishes — you will always have a transparent line-item quote before any work begins.

Do you handle both residential and commercial projects?

Yes — apartments, villas and bungalows, as well as offices and commercial spaces.

What’s included in turnkey execution?

Turnkey means we carry the whole job — design, materials and procurement, all on-site work and trades, project management and supervision, then styling and handover. You get a single point of accountability and a dated plan you can hold us to, instead of coordinating vendors yourself.

Our story

Since 2014, Pune

Two friends who never stopped arguing about doorways.

One of us draws it. One of us builds it. Between those two stubborn opinions sits every home we have handed over.

How it started

It started with an argument.

We met in design school, sharing a studio table and disagreeing about something that felt very important at the time: where a kitchen window should go.

Neither of us remembers who was right. We remember that we kept talking about it long after the class was over.

That was probably the beginning of Twin Space Studio.

Our first projects were far from glamorous. A friend's rental. A family home in Kothrud. A small dentist's waiting room. We measured spaces ourselves, drew late into the night, chased materials, and spent more evenings with carpenters than we probably should have.

And somewhere in those early projects, we learned something that stayed with us.

A great design is only as good as the way it is built.

A beautiful drawing can look perfect on a screen and completely fall apart on site. A wardrobe can have the right proportions but still feel wrong when the hinge is in the wrong place. The smallest execution detail can change how an entire room feels.

So we decided not to separate the two.

We built Twin Space Studio around a simple idea: the people designing your home should care just as much about how it is built.

That meant staying involved beyond the drawings. Sitting with fabricators. Checking materials. Solving problems on site. Reworking details when something didn't feel right. And, sometimes, arguing with each other all over again.

Today, the projects are bigger, the team has grown, and the tools have changed.

But the way we work hasn't.

We still believe the best homes come from being close to the process — listening carefully, designing with purpose, and staying involved until the last detail is right.

Because for us, Twin Space Studio was never just about making beautiful spaces.

It was about building them properly. Together.
